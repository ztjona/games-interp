# Position-dataset integrity audit: champTa and champS4 are on the wrong distribution (2026-08-11)

Status: frozen self-contained record. Parent ledger: [`phase-3.md`](phase-3.md).
Metric/method definitions: [`../methods-reference.md`](../methods-reference.md).
All findings below are `[DIRECT]` from provenance dicts and tensor shapes on
disk; the impact assessment is `[AI-REASONED PROVISIONAL ANALYSIS]`.

Triggered while building the unified 677k pool: champTa's dataset was 88,524
positions against champVe's 289,795 and champYb's 296,045, and its tiger threat
base rates were ~2x everyone else's. Both anomalies have the same cause.

## 1. What the convention promises

`Quarto-specifications.md` / `CLAUDE.md`: a champion suffix (`gorillaTa`,
`tigerVe`) means the labels were *"recomputed against that champion's self-play
position distribution so base rates match the activations"*. Each champion's
`positions-amalgam_<tag>_unique.pt` should be the dedup of **four** opponent-mode
files: `random_v_random`, `model_v_random`, `random_v_model`, `model_v_model`.

## 2. What is actually on disk [DIRECT]

| champion | positions | source files in provenance | `game` module |
|---|---:|---|---|
| champAa (`amalgam`) | 275,916 | 4 (Aa_replay) ✅ | `quarto` |
| **champS4** (`amalgam_s4`) | 275,916 | 4 — **all `Aa_replay`** ❌ | `quarto` |
| **champTa** (`amalgam_ta`) | **88,524** | **1 — `random_v_random` only** ❌ | `quarto` |
| champVe (`amalgam_ve`) | 289,795 | 4 (Ve_oracleAblation) ✅ | `quarto_s4` |
| champYb (`amalgam_yb`) | 296,045 | 4 (Yb_hotChamp) ✅ | `quarto_s4_hot` |

Two distinct defects:

- **champTa** was aggregated from `positions-random_v_random_raw.pt` *alone*.
  Its dataset contains **no positions generated by champTa** — or by any model.
  It is pure random-vs-random play. The raw file's `model_path` even points at
  champAa's checkpoint, and `game` is `quarto`, not `quarto_s4`.
- **champS4**'s file is **byte-identical** to champAa's
  (`torch.equal` on the (275916, 16, 4, 4) board tensors returns `True`). It is
  champAa's distribution under a champS4 name.

Confirmed downstream: `s4.fc1_amalgam_ta_activations.pt` is `(88524, 512)`, so
**every champTa SAE was trained on activations from random-play positions**, and
every `*Ta` BSP label tensor scores that same random-play distribution.

## 3. The data was never missing — only the aggregation step [DIRECT]

All four opponent-mode raw files exist for every champion:

```
positions-{model_v_model,model_v_random,random_v_model}-Ta_minimaxSelect_raw.pt
positions-{model_v_model,model_v_random,random_v_model}-Sa_archScan_raw.pt
```

champTa's three unused self-play files hold **263,393 raw positions**
(10,000 games each). Adding them to the `random_v_random` file and re-deduping
should land near ~290k unique, i.e. parity with Ve/Yb. **Nothing needs
regenerating**; this is a re-run of step 2 of the data pipeline.

## 4. Impact [AI-REASONED PROVISIONAL ANALYSIS]

- **The tiger base-rate gap is explained, and it is an artefact.** champTa
  `tiger_line_winnable` base rate 0.0453 vs champVe 0.0249 / champYb 0.0230.
  Random play blunders into winnable positions roughly twice as often as a
  trained agent, which avoids handing over a winning piece. This is not a
  property of champTa the model.
- **Every champTa-vs-other comparison is confounded on three axes at once**:
  distribution (random vs agent play), sample size (88k vs ~290k), and base
  rate. The champYb headline contrast — "anchored extracts conjunctions far
  better on Yb (0.60/0.81) than Ta (0.26/0.41)" — compares an SAE trained on
  *random-play* activations against one trained on *agent-play* activations. It
  is not a champion comparison as written.
- **champS4-vs-champAa comparisons are on identical positions**, which is
  *benign* for a like-for-like read (same inputs, different models — arguably
  the cleanest comparison in the project) but means champS4 has never been
  measured on its own distribution, so any claim about what champS4 *does in its
  own games* is unsupported.
- **champVe and champYb are correct** and their mutual comparison stands.
- **H9 / H11 / the 2026-05-22 tiger reframing audit** all rest partly on champTa
  numbers and need re-reading once champTa is rebuilt. The 3A dilution verdicts
  for champTa are similarly provisional — the codes are real, but the
  distribution they were measured on is not champTa's.
- **The unified 677k pool is unaffected as a pool** (dedup absorbs the duplicated
  champAa contribution), but its *composition* is skewed: of five inputs, two are
  champAa's positions and one is random play, so only Ve and Yb contribute
  agent-generated positions. Worth recomposing after the rebuild.

## 5. Fix plan

1. Re-aggregate champTa from all four raw files (`deduplicate_positions.py`),
   expect ~290k unique. Same for champS4 from the `Sa_archScan` raws.
2. Recompute `gorillaTa` / `hawkTa` / `tigerTa` (and `*S4`) labels.
3. Re-collect `s4.fc1` / `s4.conv2` activations, trained **and** random model.
4. **Retrain the champTa (and champS4) SAE sweeps** — the expensive step; the
   existing checkpoints are trained on the wrong activations.
5. Re-evaluate, then re-run 3A for those champions.
6. Rebuild the unified pool so its composition reflects the corrected inputs.

Steps 1-3 are cheap (CPU minutes + a few GPU minutes). Step 4 is a full sweep.

## 6. Prevention

`scripts/unify_positions.py` and the champion runners should assert that a
champion's amalgam names that champion's own raw files and carries the expected
`game` module — a provenance check, not a count check, since 88,524 looked
merely small rather than wrong. Added as a bite-mark in `CLAUDE.md`.
